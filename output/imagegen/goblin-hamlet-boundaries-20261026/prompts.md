# Goblin hamlet boundary obstacle proposals

Generated using built-in image_gen, transparent_background=true. Three independent PNG art candidates for visually blocking the ends of a side-scrolling map. Not final canon. No scene placement, collision, physics, movement restrictions or import settings have been added. Sprite art alone cannot stop the player; a separately configured collider will be required after a design is selected.

Alpha was checked on each generated PNG: fully transparent background samples and visible object samples. Original generated files are retained; these copies preserve alpha without postprocessing. A sprite may be mirrored for the other map edge, but lighting should be checked in-scene.

## GoblinHamlet_RockWall_v1.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent PNG blocking boundary prop for a 2D side-scrolling fantasy game. Reference 1 is placement context: the LEFT edge of a playable platform where the scenery stops; do NOT reproduce the screenshot, gray editor void, green lines or tile platform. Reference 2 is ONLY the goblin hamlet's realistic painterly fantasy style, daylight, rough materials and palette. Generate a NEW isolated environmental boundary obstacle, NOT a scene. It must read as a high, substantial impassable vertical barrier at the end of the playable ground, NOT a low jumpable decorative prop. Clear tall vertical silhouette with a broad stable level base that can sit on an existing platform. Near-front side-scroller viewpoint, slight natural depth, not isometric or overhead. Fully visible object, centered with generous transparent margin around all sides, complete base. Genuine transparent RGBA background including every exterior space, no opaque backdrop or painted checkerboard. Match earthy brown/gray tones, weathering and upper-left warm daylight of the references. No people or goblins, no text, no symbols, no UI, no floor, landscape or ground slab. No doors, tunnels, windows, paths, usable ladders or staircase-like ledges. A reusable cutout that can be mirrored for the opposite end of the map. Subject: a massive tall natural rock outcrop forming a compact vertical cliff wall. Dense contiguous craggy gray-brown rock mass, steep sheer face, sparse weathered cracks and a little moss tucked into cracks, rugged uneven crown. Flat planted base, near-vertical sides with very little outward slope, no stepped boulder pile and no climbable terraces. The obstacle's height substantially exceeds its base width. Portrait composition.
